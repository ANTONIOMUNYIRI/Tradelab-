## How a trade works

## Learn before you trade

What is a binary option?

A binary option is an all-or-nothing contract on whether a price will be above or below its starting level when a timer ends. The stake and the possible payout are fixed in advance, so there is no partial win.

Why is it hard to come out ahead?

A win pays less than the stake you risk, so you must win more than half your trades just to break even. Use the calculator to see how much more.

How do I avoid scams?

Be wary of anyone promising guaranteed profits, “bots” that win every time, pressure to deposit quickly, or withdrawals that are delayed or blocked. In Kenya, only firms licensed by the Capital Markets Authority may offer online forex trading, so check the CMA’s register before sending money to anyone.

How much should I risk per trade?

Most risk guidance suggests keeping each stake to a small share of your balance, often 1 to 2 percent, so a losing streak does not wipe you out. Try it here first and ask the AI coach to check your risk.
